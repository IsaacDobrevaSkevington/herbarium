Outdoors and Unbothered: Herbs for Seasonal Allergies with Selima Harleston Lust

Do you love being outdoors, but feel like your body is at war with grass, trees, and weeds? In this comprehensive masterclass, you’ll learn how to support your immune system and ease respiratory allergy symptoms using trusted plant allies.
All Masterclasses

Class Materials

Video Transcript (PDF)

Class Description:

In this in-depth masterclass with clinical herbalist and Herbal Academy Lead Herbalist Educator Selima Harleston Lust, we’ll explore how allergies develop, why certain people are more sensitive, and what truly effective herbal support looks like, so you can enjoy being outside instead of feeling trapped indoors.

You’ll get familiar with key plant allies to stock your home apothecary with, and learn a few herbal blends, lifestyle shifts, and nutrition tips that help reduce histamine reactions, support detoxification, and keep your sinuses open all season long.

You’ll also gain insight into the risks of over-the-counter medications, how to use herbs alongside pharmaceuticals if needed, and how to build a practical, seasonal plan—including strategies for kids.

Whether you’re sneezing at the first sign of spring or dreading fall pollen, this class offers a holistic toolkit to help you breathe easier, feel better, and enjoy nature without hesitation.

Instructor Bio:

Selima Harleston Lust is a clinical herbalist and medical astrologer, serving as a Lead Herbalist Educator at the Herbal Academy. Selima is also the co-founder of Iwilla Remedy, where she blends plant-based healing with intuitive guidance to support personal and collective transformation. Selima is passionate about helping people travel the most graceful path from ill to well with a holistic approach that bridges mind, body, and spirit. Her work centers on empowering individuals to reclaim their health and personal power, particularly after trauma. Learn more about her work at theherbalacademy.com and iwillaremedy.com.
