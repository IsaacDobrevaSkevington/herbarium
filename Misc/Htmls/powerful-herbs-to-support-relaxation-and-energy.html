Powerful Herbs to Support Relaxation and Energy

  • The Herbarium Team
  • November 24, 2020

In this video presentation, join Herbal Academy’s Heather Irvine for a lively talk on the topic of herbs that support relaxation and energy. Heather shares numerous ideas for incorporating a few favorite adaptogenic herbs into a regular routine for overall stress management. She talks about how and why herbs can be such a great support to boost energy and reduce the effects of stress, with special focus on adaptogen and nervine herbs lemon balm, eleuthero, shatavari, sage, and tulsi.

This video is a joint webinar between Herbal Academy and our friends at Ultimate Bundles. The Ultimate Bundles folks put together bundles on all different topics and niches, from health and wellness to business, including The Herbs & Essential Oils Super Bundle, one of the most popular, which Herbal Academy has enjoyed contributing to year after year. The team at Ultimate Bundles worked with the contributors of the 2020 Herbs & Essential Oils Super Bundle to put together a fabulous natural remedies eBook, which you can download for free here.
