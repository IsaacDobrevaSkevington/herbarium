Session 5: Lactation Support

60 minutes

Introduction

In this session with herbalist and doula Jovan Sage, we’ll look at the anatomical and physiological basics of breastfeeding, including the emotional complexities that can accompany it and the nuances of each individual breastfeeding pair. The importance of knowledge, emotional support, and communication between a nursing pair, as well as between the feeding parent and support persons, is highlighted. 

We’ll explore herbal preparations and lifestyle strategies that can ease and help with some of the most common breastfeeding concerns, from mastitis to sore nipples, and share a number of informative resources for further troubleshooting and support, no matter what a specific nursing journey entails.

Conclusion

Nursing is a uniquely individual choice that is different for every parent-child pairing. Herbs offer support for both over- and under-production of breastmilk, along with a range of other challenging scenarios, making them a wonderful addition to a breastfeeding journey.
